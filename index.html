Frequently Asked Questions

Which device is right for my office?

Tell us your monthly print volume, number of users and the types of documents you print. We’ll recommend a dependable model and plan that fits your needs.

Are toner and ink included?

Yes. During the rental term, essential supplies such as ink, toner and drums are included (paper excluded). We monitor usage and replenish supplies to help prevent interruptions.

How are rental plans tailored?

We recommend a plan based on your equipment needs, monthly print volume and rental term.

When should I use remote support?

Remote support can help with software issues such as installing printer drivers on a new computer or reconnecting a scan folder.
